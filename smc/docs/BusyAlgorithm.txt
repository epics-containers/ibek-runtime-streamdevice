The unit is made to ramp by changing the target level.
This can be inhibited by having the pause button on.
Thus it can also be made to ramp by turning the pause button off (if there has been a change in the other parameters).

There will be a delay until the status records are updated.
But then the STS:RAMP:MADE record will say whether the unit is ramping or not.
Ramp Target Made
or Ramping.
However if the pause is on, the record will say made, not ramping.



The target level button changes.
The pause button is off.
The state is ramping.
